**Privacy Policy for VocabApp**

**Last Updated:** 20 December, 2025

Thank you for using VocabApp. Your privacy is important to us.

**Data Collection and Usage**

VocabApp is designed to be a private and offline-first experience. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

All the data you create in the app, including your vocabulary lists, words, and meanings, is stored exclusively in a local database on your own device. This data is not accessible to us or any third party and is never sent over the internet.

**Permissions**

VocabApp does not require any permissions that would allow it to access personal information from your device.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
